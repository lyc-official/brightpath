What does three-quarters mean?

Split the whole into equal parts. Then take the parts needed.

Primary Maths · Fractions

Find ¾ of 28.

7777
  1. Split 28 into four equal groups.28 ÷ 4 = 7
  2. Take three of those groups.7 × 3 = 21

¾ of 28 is 21.
